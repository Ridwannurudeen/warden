What an audit record contains

The target and fixed battery define scope. Grade, score, and blocked or flagged cases describe that run. The integrity state reports whether the stored payload still matches its server-side signature, and the issue time bounds the observation.

This is point-in-time evidence, not certification. A valid stored-record signature establishes integrity for the displayed payload under the Warden instance's configured badge key. It does not establish current endpoint safety, target ownership, continuous protection, or coverage beyond the tested battery.
