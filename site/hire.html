If your task freezes in accepted: a task created with x402 payment mode may come back with [x402 Replay Failed] HTTP 405. That is OKX's automatic replay failing on their side, not a Warden outage — the endpoint answers a normal task-402-pay call. Nothing was charged. Run step 2 above against a freshly fetched 402 challenge and the task settles.
