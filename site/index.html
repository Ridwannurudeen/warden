Verifiable pre-action security

Stop poisoned agent output before it becomes an action.

Warden sits between untrusted agent output and consequential execution. It returns ALLOW, SANITIZE, or BLOCK, withholds or transforms unsafe actions, and can produce evidence that can be independently inspected.

              What was checked
              
The supplied payload and the context implemented by the caller.

            

            

              What was not checked
              
Every possible risk outside the implemented detector boundary.

            

            

              Justified conclusion
              

                BLOCK means withhold the proposed action. SANITIZE means review
                the transformed payload. ALLOW means no implemented detector
                fired—not that the content is universally safe.
              

            

          

          

            Known patterns are covered by deterministic and corpus-backed
            layers. Novel phrasing may require the separately configured paid
            semantic path and can still be missed.
          

        


        

          

            

              
One boundary, four guarantees

              
Scan. Attest. Verify. Audit.

              implying universal safety.
