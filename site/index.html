One boundary, four guarantees

              
Scan. Attest. Verify. Audit.

            

            

              Every dangerous payload follows the same pipeline: caught before
              the action, recorded with a signature, checkable by anyone,
              measurable on demand.
            

          

          

            

              
                
              
              
Scan

              

                Gate one untrusted payload before a payment, tool call, link,
                or secret-handling action — ALLOW, SANITIZE, or BLOCK.
              

            

            

              
                
              
              
Attest

              

                Every non-ALLOW verdict is issued as an Ed25519-signed
                attestation and appended to a hash-chained transparency log.
              

            

            

              
                
              
              
Verify

              

                Anyone re-checks the signature and chain in the browser with
                WebCrypto — no server, no account, no trust in Warden required.
              

            

            

              
                
              
              
Audit

              

                Run the fixed attack battery against an endpoint you are
                authorized to test and receive a graded, signed record.
