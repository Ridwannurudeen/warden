Decision contract

One response. Four separate questions.

The integrating agent should not collapse verdict, severity, evidence, and coverage into one green or red label.

ALLOW

Continue under policy

No implemented detector fired for this request. This is not proof that the content is safe.

SANITIZE

Use the safe output or review

Warden removed or reshaped risky material. A heuristic link can be sanitized even when the score maps to risk NONE.

BLOCK

Stop the next action

A hard gate or composite signal crossed the enforcement boundary. Do not execute the original payload.
