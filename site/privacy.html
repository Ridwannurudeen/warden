Privacy Summary

Warden does not request, store, or sell personally identifiable information from visitors. Requests made to scan endpoints include only the payload text and optional context fields needed for analysis.

  • Audit records are retained only for badge lookup and are identified by audit id.
  • Log fields may include request metadata required for service operation.
  • For paid requests, payment metadata is handled by the configured payment gateway.
  • Warden is an independent service and does not claim endorsement by OKX; paid usage terms are defined by this project.
