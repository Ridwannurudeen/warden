Decision model

Read the operational decision before the diagnostic detail.

Decision

ALLOW, SANITIZE, or BLOCK tells the integrating agent what to do next.

Risk

The observed severity band comes from the deterministic score and hard gates. It is not the decision itself.

Reason and threat class

A reason code names the detector that fired. The response's threat_classes array deduplicates those reasons.

Confidence and score

Detection confidence and the composite score are evidence used by the decision engine, not probabilities of safety.

Detector boundary

Fast and thorough depth do not run identical checks. CORPUS_MATCH is thorough-only.

Detected threats start at risk MEDIUM: a finding that changes or withholds an action is never presented as low severity. Decision answers what to do; risk communicates the calibrated review floor.
